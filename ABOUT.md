## 22 years

The arc runs from solving other people's cable complaints to calculating the laying conditions for
one of the first transmission connected batteries in the UK.

**Cable and Wireless, Bulldog Communications.** Rolling out LLU broadband, where I managed
customer complaints about broadband cable and billing issues, and solved complex complaints.

**Lapp Group UK**, a Stuttgart, Germany based international cable group specialised in industrial
applications. I spent 2 years based in the London office supporting field sales engineers across
the South West, the Midlands and Wales, 2 years in external field sales as a sales engineer in the
South East of England, and 1 year as National Business Development Manager, where I pioneered the
establishment of solar and wind energy business units in the UK and led new sales campaigns,
product development, procurement and product management initiatives.

**Honeywell Safety Products.** I trained Vestas and Siemens as well as RWE technicians in safety at
height, climbing and evacuation systems, and held product management responsibility to build on the
success in renewables I had brought from Lapp Cables UK via Lapp Group.

**Ventus Ltd.** Since 2012 I have run my own business, and became Global Key Accounts for LEONI AG
in solar. I originated SOLARpower Alu ATA cables in cooperation with Mr Christoph Studer and Mr
Wolfgang Kessler, which became an enormous success as the first dedicated product for DC and AC
distributed solar applications, introducing radial water blocking and new techniques in the derating
of cables.

That work led to success at Stocking Pelham Battery Storage, where derating calculations drawing on
my industrial and utility cable experience established the laying conditions for 80 x 400 mm² DC
cables, for one of the first transmission connected batteries in the UK. I have extensively
documented this origination at https://globalgrid2050.com/employers_requirements_BESS/, where I
received immensely positive feedback from installer Mr David King, who installed the project for SMA
and British Solar Renewables as Sphere Electrical. Mr David King's photo is credited in the write up.

I then took these skills to the successful completion of one of the world's first EV forecourts at
Braintree, documented at https://globalgrid2050.com/power_systems_studies/, as well as early subsidy
free solar projects funding support via the CTO of Gridserve and project engineers at York Solar
Farm, where I learned the new risks posed by higher powered modules and the complexity in solar PV
design understanding.

That led to completion of my first 110 kV HV connection with TenneT at the Nordspolder 96 MWp
project with Ampyr Solar Europe.

## What did not work

I bid 132 kV at UKPN, made the final two, and lost it when the supply chain broke down in Turkey at
HES Kablo. I then travelled to China, bid, and lost that one too, learning business cultures in Asia
in the process. Through Brexit, Covid and the Ukraine war I persevered, and won back major solar
projects in the UK at the height of Iran war uncertainty.

Those losses are recorded here deliberately. A record that only lists wins cannot be checked, and
this project is built on the principle that a claim which cannot be checked is not yet a fact.

## Why build GLOBALGRID2050 now

I want to use this experience to BUILD www.globalgrid2050.com in the age of AI, to support efforts
preventing irreversible climate destruction, rising inflation and energy security issues.

I follow the conclusion of the third Terawatt Workshop, held in Germany in May 2022, that 75 TW of
installed solar capacity by 2050 is a realistic global target. That conclusion came from the
workshop discussions combined with a wide ranging review of decarbonisation pathways, energy demand
projections and the state of the art in PV technology, and was set out by more than 50 leading
figures from the PV industry. Reported by pv magazine on 1 July 2023:
https://www.pv-magazine.com/2023/07/01/weekend-read-waiting-is-not-an-option/

A separate projection holds that solar may cover as much as 75 per cent of global electricity demand
by 2050, reported by pv magazine on 13 April 2021:
https://www.pv-magazine.com/2021/04/13/solar-may-cover-75-of-global-electricity-demand-by-2050/
These are two different quantities, installed capacity and share of demand. They share a number and
nothing else, and they are not to be merged.

I understand that the solar field has suffered from almost two decades of boom and bust cycles.

Given my industrial, infrastructure, solar, wind, EV and battery storage and power systems
engineering experience, supported by chartered engineers with indemnity, I believe the age of AI has
dawned, and that large language models may allow complex grid, climate, financial and power
systems models that materially reduce energy use.

The numbers behind that case, and the peak and diversity argument that follows from them, are in
[EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md).

## Hypotheses this project exists to test

These are not results. They are the questions the work is pointed at, written down so that they can
be proved wrong.

1. **That modelling can cut energy use by as much as 70 per cent.** I have stated this figure on the
   podcast. It carries no evidence yet and should be read as a hypothesis until it does.
2. **That peak and diversity, not annual energy, are the binding constraint** on electrifying heat
   and transport in the UK. The case is set out in [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md), and it is checkable.
3. **That a visual, spatial working mode finishes systems a written one abandons.** Kuiper-belt is
   the instrument built to test it, and its own score, the count of bodies nobody can open, is the
   measurement.

## Why open source, and why self funded

I have since very early years been a systems thinker, a curious business executive and a sci-fi
superfan, and I believe education of the public and the world is necessary, and the duty of humanity
to progress human civilisation. I have taken on, in the last 2 years, enormous pains to codify all
of this experience, and the experience of my own business Ventus Ltd, into the GLOBALGRID2050.com
project.

I hope that by doing the work open source and funding it myself, I can remain independent and, with
integrity, play my small part in supporting future generations to be successful, in order to honour
all that I have been lucky to be a part of in the journey above.
