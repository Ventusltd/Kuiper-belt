## 22 years

The arc runs from solving other people's cable complaints to calculating the laying conditions for
one of the first transmission connected batteries in the UK.

**Cable and Wireless, Bulldog Communications.** Rolling out LLU broadband, where he managed
customer complaints about broadband cable and billing issues, and solved complex complaints.

**Lapp Group UK**, a Stuttgart, Germany based international cable group specialised in industrial
applications. He spent 2 years based in the London office supporting field sales engineers across
the South West, the Midlands and Wales, 2 years in external field sales as a sales engineer in the
South East of England, and 1 year as National Business Development Manager, where he pioneered the
establishment of solar and wind energy business units in the UK and led new sales campaigns, product development,
procurement and product management initiatives.

**Honeywell Safety Products.** He trained Vestas and Siemens as well as RWE technicians in safety at
height, climbing and evacuation systems, and held product management responsibility to build on this
success in renewables from Lapp Cables UK via Lapp Group.

**Ventus Ltd.** Since 2012 he has run his own business, and became Global Key Accounts for LEONI AG
in solar. He originated SOLARpower Alu ATA cables in cooperation with Mr Christoph Studer and Mr
Wolfgang Kessler, which became an enormous success as the first dedicated product for DC and AC
distributed solar applications, introducing radial water blocking and new techniques in the derating
of cables.

That work led to success at Stocking Pelham Battery Storage, where derating calculations drawing on
his industrial and utility cable experience established the laying conditions for 80 x 400 mm² DC
cables, for one of the first transmission connected batteries in the UK. He has extensively
documented this origination at https://globalgrid2050.com/employers_requirements_BESS/, where he
received immensely positive feedback from installer Mr David King, who installed the project for SMA
and British Solar Renewables as Sphere Electrical. Mr David King's photo is credited in the write up.

He then took these skills to the successful completion of one of the world's first EV forecourts at
Braintree, documented at https://globalgrid2050.com/power_systems_studies/, as well as early subsidy
free solar projects funding support via the CTO of Gridserve and project engineers at York Solar
Farm, where he learned the new risks posed by higher powered modules and the complexity in solar PV
design understanding.

That led to completion of his first 110 kV HV connection with TenneT at the Nordspolder 96 MWp
project with Ampyr Solar Europe.

## What did not work

He bid 132 kV at UKPN, made the final two, and lost it when the supply chain broke down in Turkey at
HES Kablo. He then travelled to China, bid, and lost that one too, learning business cultures in
Asia in the process. Through Brexit, Covid and the Ukraine war he persevered, and won back major
solar projects in the UK at the height of Iran war uncertainty.

Those losses are recorded here deliberately. A record that only lists wins cannot be checked, and
this project is built on the principle that a claim which cannot be checked is not yet a fact.

## Why build GLOBALGRID2050 now

He wants to use this experience to BUILD www.globalgrid2050.com in the age of AI, to support efforts
preventing irreversible climate destruction, rising inflation and energy security issues. He follows
projections that solar may cover as much as 75 per cent of global electricity demand by 2050, as
reported by pv magazine on 13 April 2021:
https://www.pv-magazine.com/2021/04/13/solar-may-cover-75-of-global-electricity-demand-by-2050/

He has separately cited a deployment figure in the order of 75 TWp as the scale that may be
required. That is a different claim in a different unit, installed capacity rather than share of
demand, and it is listed below as a candidate until its own source is attached. He understands that
the solar field has suffered from almost two decades of boom and bust cycles.

Given his industrial, infrastructure, solar, wind, EV and battery storage and power systems
engineering experience, supported by chartered engineers with indemnity, he believes the age of AI
has dawned, and that large language models may allow complex grid, climate, financial and power
systems models that materially reduce energy use. He has stated on the podcast that the reduction
could be as much as 70 per cent. That figure is a hypothesis this project exists to test, not a
result it has produced, and it should be read as such until it carries evidence.

For scale, he has been on record in the podcast stating that the Office for National Statistics
recorded total primary energy usage in the UK of approximately 1,600 TWh, using the IEA conversion
of 11.63 to turn million tonnes of oil equivalent into terawatt hours. The point of translating the
figure is to communicate the challenges facing the UK power grid: to grow for national and social
security, and to control runaway inflation and climate change.

He has set out that argument in detail in his own published study, The real peak challenge of
electrifying heat and transport in the UK:
https://www.ventusltd.com/post/the-real-peak-challenge-of-electrifying-heat-and-transport-in-the-uk

## Why open source, and why self funded

Vikram Kumar has since very early years been a systems thinker, a curious business executive and a
sci-fi superfan, and believes education of the public and the world is necessary, and the duty of
humanity to progress human civilisation. He has taken on, in the last 2 years, enormous pains to
codify all of this experience, and the experience of his own business Ventus Ltd, into the
GLOBALGRID2050.com project.

He hopes that by doing the work open source and funding it himself, he can remain independent and,
with integrity, play his small part in supporting future generations to be successful, in order to
honour all that he has been lucky to be a part of in the journey above.
